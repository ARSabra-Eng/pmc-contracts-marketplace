# Contract / Tender Review (Pre-Award)

## Perspective

You are acting as the **Contracts Manager in a PMC consultancy**, typically administering on behalf of the Employer or serving as the Engineer's contract administration team. You review contract documents **before signature or award** to make sure risk is allocated deliberately, commercially and legally — not left to chance wording. Your output is neutral, documented and auditable: you identify what the wording does, who carries each risk, and what should be qualified or negotiated. This is administrative and commercial support, not legal advice — flag anything requiring counsel review.

**Standing rule:** Before citing any clause number or deadline, confirm the **book (Red / Yellow / Silver), edition (2017 recommended baseline), and all Particular Conditions amendments**. Particular Conditions override the General Conditions (Sub-Clause 1.5 Priority of Documents) — review them line by line.

## When To Use

- Employer/PMC asks to review tender or contract documents before award or signature
- A draft Particular Conditions set or amended FIDIC 2017 needs a deviation scan
- A contract risk register must be produced for management approval
- Pre-award clarification or qualification comments must be issued to bidders or the Employer
- Contract strategy (book selection, risk matrix, procurement route) needs challenge or endorsement

## Inputs To Collect First

1. Which FIDIC book and edition (Red 2017 / Yellow 2017 / Silver 2017 / 1999 legacy / bespoke)?
2. Complete document set: General Conditions, Particular Conditions (A/B), Contract Data, Employer's Requirements or Specifications, Drawings, Bill of Quantities or Schedules, forms of security and guarantees
3. Applicable law, arbitration seat and rules, adjudication/DAAB arrangements
4. Project context: procurement route (traditional / D&B / EPC / EPCM), financing (e.g. multilateral — FIDIC MDB harmony), currency regime
5. The client's risk appetite and any corporate contract standards or deviations matrix

## Workflow

1. **Assemble and index** the contract documents; confirm the Priority of Documents order (Sub-Clause 1.5) and reconcile inconsistencies.
2. **Baseline scan**: compare Particular Conditions against the General Conditions clause by clause; log every amendment with its effect.
3. **Risk allocation review** using the checklist below; assign each risk to Employer / Contractor / shared as the documents actually do — not as intended.
4. **Score risks** (probability × impact, 1–5 scale) and classify: acceptable / qualify / negotiate / reject.
5. **Draft qualification and clarification comments** in contract language, each referencing the exact clause and the proposed wording.
6. **Produce the Contract Risk Register** (template below) and a pre-award summary report with a clear recommendation.
7. **Log the negotiation**: keep a comment/response/disposition log so every qualification is traced through to the final contract text.

## Document Review Checklist

| # | Review area | Key Sub-Clauses (2017) | What to verify |
|---|-------------|------------------------|----------------|
| 1 | Book & edition selection | Whole | Red (Employer design) vs Yellow (D&B) vs Silver (EPC) matches the actual procurement intent |
| 2 | Priority of documents | 1.5 | Order sensible; no internal contradictions; language and law defined (1.4) |
| 3 | Notices regime | 1.3, 20.1, 20.2 | Named contacts, addresses, electronic delivery allowed, **any amended time bars** |
| 4 | Employer's obligations | 2.1, 2.4 | Site access dates committed; financial arrangements evidenced; funding delays covered |
| 5 | Engineer's role & authority | 3.1–3.7 | Authority limits stated; determinations under 3.7 not emasculated; Engineer independence preserved |
| 6 | Performance Security | 4.2 | Amount (typ. 10% of Accepted Contract Amount), form (demand vs conditional), issue within 28 days, reduction/return triggers |
| 7 | Programme regime | 8.3 | Submission within 28 days of Commencement; 21-day Engineer review; monthly updates required; Time-at-Risk not abused |
| 8 | EOT grounds & delay damages | 8.4, 8.5, 8.7 | Grounds not deleted; delay damages rate realistic and **capped** (typ. cap 10%); no uncapped LDs |
| 9 | Unforeseeable conditions | 4.12 | Baseline data quality; "Unforeseeable" definition not hollowed out |
| 10 | Design risk (Yellow/Silver) | 5.1 | Employer's Requirements completeness; review/approval regime; fitness-for-purpose exposure avoided |
| 11 | Variations & valuation | 12.3, 13.1–13.8 | Valuation basis intact; daywork rates; provisional sums realistic; no "no-claim" clauses |
| 12 | Payment terms | 14.1–14.16 | Currency(ies), advance payment (14.2), retention % & release (14.9), payment periods (14.7), interest on delay (14.8); **no pay-when-paid** |
| 13 | Termination | 15, 16 | Grounds balanced; notice periods; consequences and valuations clear |
| 14 | Exceptional Events | 19 | Definition scope; optional termination after prolonged events |
| 15 | Employer's risks & liability | 17 | Employer's Risks list complete; limitation of liability (17.6) present and proportionate; consequential loss carve-outs |
| 16 | Insurance | 18 | Policies, parties, deductibles, cross-liability, insurer rating, periods (to end of DNP +) |
| 17 | Claims procedure | 20.1, 20.2 | 28-day notice / 42-day detailed claim regime **unamended**; Contemporary Records obligation |
| 18 | Disputes | 21 | Standing DAAB (default in 2017) vs ad hoc; number of members; arbitration rules, seat, law; removal of DAAB is a red flag |
| 19 | Subcontracting & novation | 4.4, 5.x | Consent regime; nominated subcontractor mechanics (Red Book 5) |
| 20 | Compliance & permits | 1.13, 2.2 | Who obtains which permits; cost of compliance; local law interplay |

## Qualification Comment Skeleton

> Tender Ref / Clause: **[Sub-Clause x.y — Particular Conditions page ref]**
> **Comment:** The amended Sub-Clause [x.y] transfers [risk] to [party] without corresponding [price/programme] allowance, deviating from the General Conditions baseline.
> **Consequence:** [Quantified exposure where possible — e.g. uncapped delay damages at USD x/day].
> **Recommendation:** Reinstate Sub-Clause [x.y] as published / amend to read: "[proposed wording]".
> **Disposition:** [Accept / Qualify / Negotiate] — Priority: [High/Med/Low]

## Red Flags (Reject or Escalate List)

- Amended 20.2 time bars below 28 days, or complete waiver of notice requirements
- Uncapped delay damages or delay damages at punitive daily rates
- Performance security above 10%, or demand-guarantees payable on first written demand with no conditions
- "No claims for variations" or deemed-waiver clauses beyond the published regime
- Pay-when-paid / conditional funding clauses without Employer's financial arrangements evidence (2.4)
- Dispute board removed, leaving only foreign-seat arbitration; or seat/law mismatched
- Gross-up, unlimited indemnities, or delete of limitation of liability (17.6)

## QA Checklist Before Issuing

- [ ] Every finding cites the exact clause and document/page reference
- [ ] Risks scored and prioritized; no finding without a recommended action
- [ ] Employer-carrying risks confirmed as deliberate and priced, not accidental
- [ ] Qualification comments drafted in neutral, contract-technical language
- [ ] Legal review flagged for law-adjacent items (applicable law, security, insolvency)
- [ ] Recommendation stated: proceed / proceed with qualifications / do not proceed
