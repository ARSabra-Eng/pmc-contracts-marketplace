# Contract Mobilization & Commencement (Post-Award)

## Perspective

Act as the **PMC/Engineer** converting the awarded contract into a functioning administration system before site work starts. Verify every instrument, particular, date, and Notice; preserve an auditable record. This is administrative support, not legal advice.

**Standing rule:** The detailed rules below are the **FIDIC Red Book 2017 General Conditions baseline**. Confirm the book, edition, Particular Conditions, and Contract Data before acting. Days are calendar days unless the Contract states otherwise.

## When To Use

- The Letter of Acceptance has been received and the Contract must be executed and mobilized.
- The Commencement Date must be fixed and notified.
- The initial or revised programme requires review under Sub-Clause 8.3.
- Performance Security, Insurances, or the advance payment guarantee must be checked.
- Contract particulars, correspondence protocols, and administration registers must be established.

## Inputs To Collect First

1. Letter of Acceptance and the complete Contract, including Particular Conditions and Contract Data.
2. Signed Contract Agreement, when available, and all agreed amendments.
3. Performance Security, insurance policies/certificates, and advance payment guarantee.
4. Employer's financial arrangements evidence under Sub-Clause 2.4, where requested.
5. Named representatives, Notice addresses, and permitted electronic systems.

## Workflow

1. **Verify execution documents (1.5/1.6).** Reconcile the Contract Agreement, Letter of Acceptance, Accepted Contract Amount, scope, Particular Conditions, and Contract Data. Ensure agreed amendments are incorporated into the Contract rather than left only in correspondence.
2. **Check Performance Security (4.2).** Verify receipt within 28 days after the Contractor receives the Letter of Acceptance, and check amount, form, issuer, validity, and Contract Data requirements. Record reduction and return events without treating a typical percentage as a contractual default.
3. **Check Insurances (Clause 19).** Verify the required insured parties, risks, limits, deductibles, evidence, and effective periods against Clause 19 and Contract Data. Record non-compliance and required corrective action.
4. **Administer advance payment prerequisites (14.2).** Confirm that advance payment applies, the required guarantee and Performance Security are in place, and the advance payment certificate and repayment schedule follow Sub-Clause 14.2 and Contract Data.
5. **Notify the Commencement Date (8.1).** The Engineer gives Notice not less than **14 days** before the Commencement Date. Unless the Particular Conditions state otherwise, the Commencement Date is within **42 days** after the Contractor receives the Letter of Acceptance. Diarize the Time for Completion under Sub-Clause 8.2.
6. **Review the initial programme (8.3).** The Contractor submits the initial programme within **28 days** after receiving the Notice under Sub-Clause 8.1. Review logic, sequencing, critical path, access and information dates, methods, resources, key dates, and contractual obligations. The Engineer has 21 days after receiving the initial programme to give a Notice identifying any non-compliance; otherwise, a Notice of No-objection is deemed given. For a revised programme, the corresponding period is 14 days after receiving a revised programme. Use **Notice of No-objection**, not “approval,” and do not imply that the Contractor is relieved of obligations.
7. **Establish correspondence and Notice controls (1.3/1.4).** Record named recipients, addresses, language, permitted electronic delivery, numbering, and the Notices register.
8. **Publish the Contract Particulars Summary.** Reconcile Time for Completion, Delay Damages under 8.8, DNP, retention, advance payment, DAAB, arbitration, law, and other project-specific entries to the Contract.
9. **Issue the administration manual.** Include registers, delegated authority, communication routes, meeting and reporting calendars, document control, and escalation rules.
10. **Hold the mobilization meeting.** Minute protocols, subcontractor controls under 5.1/5.2, permits, access, programme requirements, and Contemporary Records under 20.2.3.

## Programme Review Notice Skeleton

> Programme reference: **[revision/date]**, submitted under Sub-Clause 8.3.
>
> **Findings:** [numbered non-compliance findings with activity and clause references].
>
> **Engineer's Notice:** [identify the extent of non-compliance, or issue a Notice of No-objection]. The Contractor shall submit any required revised programme under Sub-Clause 8.3.
>
> **Reservation:** A Notice of No-objection does not relieve the Contractor of its obligations.

## QA Checklist

- [ ] Securities, guarantees, and policies reconcile to the Contract and expiry actions are diarized.
- [ ] The 8.1 Notice and Commencement Date satisfy both contractual timing rules.
- [ ] The initial programme trigger and 21-day/14-day review periods are correctly recorded.
- [ ] Programme correspondence uses Notice of No-objection terminology.
- [ ] Contract particulars reconcile to the signed Contract, including Delay Damages under 8.8.
- [ ] Registers, delegation, correspondence controls, and Contemporary Records under 20.2.3 are operational.
