# Contract Mobilization & Commencement (Post-Award)

## Perspective

You are the **PMC/Engineer** converting the awarded contract into a functioning administration system before site work starts. Errors made at mobilization — wrong particulars recorded, securities not validated, programme accepted without review — surface as disputes a year later. Work from checklists, verify every security and date, and issue everything in writing under Sub-Clause 1.3. Administrative support, not legal advice.

**Standing rule:** All periods are calendar days under the 2017 editions unless Particular Conditions state otherwise. Verify each deadline against the contract's own Contract Data before acting.

## When To Use

- Letter of Acceptance received and the contract must be executed and mobilized
- Commencement Date must be fixed and notified
- Baseline programme must be reviewed and accepted/rejected (Sub-Clause 8.3)
- Performance security, insurances, and advance payment guarantee must be checked
- The contract administration manual, contact protocols and registers must be set up

## Inputs To Collect First

1. Letter of Acceptance and final negotiated contract set (signed Contract Agreement if available)
2. Contract Data (both Parts) and Contract Agreement particulars
3. Draft performance security, insurance certificates/policies, advance payment guarantee
4. Employer's internal approvals and funding evidence (2.4)
5. Named representatives and notice addresses for all three parties (Employer, Engineer, Contractor)

## Workflow

1. **Verify execution documents**: Contract Agreement signed by all parties (1.6); Letter of Acceptance scope and Accepted Contract Amount consistent with Contract Data; any agreed amendments carried into Particular Conditions — never left in correspondence.
2. **Check Performance Security (4.2)**: received within 28 days of the Letter of Acceptance (or Contract Data period); amount = Contract Data value (typ. 10% of Accepted Contract Amount); form, issuer and expiry acceptable; reduction triggers recorded; diary its return date.
3. **Check Insurances (Clause 18)**: policies in the joint names / parties required; cover types and amounts per 18.2–18.4; deductibles acceptable; validity from Commencement through Defects Notification Period; obtain copies before allowing commencement; record non-compliance consequences (4.2 / 18.1).
4. **Advance Payment (14.2)**: confirm Contract Data provides for it; advance payment guarantee received and adequate; payment due within the contract period after both security and guarantee are received; set the amortization recovery schedule (via 14.6(c)).
5. **Fix and notify the Commencement Date (8.1)**: issue the notice within 28 days after the Engineer receives full performance security compliance unless Contract Data sets another date; diarize the Time for Completion it triggers (8.2).
6. **Review the baseline programme (8.3)**: Contractor must submit within 28 days of the Commencement Date. Review for: logic, critical path, key dates (access, drawings, approvals), obligations mapped to activities, methods/_general arrangements, Time-at-Risk transparency, and resources histogram. The Engineer gives notice of the extent of non-acceptance within 21 days (2017), with reasons; request resubmission — never "approved, subject to" ambiguities. Record the review clock.
7. **Establish the correspondence & notice protocol (1.3)**: named recipients, addresses, language (1.4), permitted electronic delivery, document numbering convention, and the notices register (see skill `contract-administration`).
8. **Verify Contract Data particulars** against the signed agreement: Time for Completion, delay damages rate and cap, DNP length, retention % and release schedule, advance payment, key dates, DAAB details, arbitration seat/rules/law. Publish the one-page **Contract Particulars Summary** to the project team.
9. **Issue the mobilization/contract administration manual**: registers list, delegation of Engineer authority matrix, correspondence matrix, meeting calendar, reporting calendar, document control rules.
10. **Kick-off administration meeting**: minute the protocol, agree the subcontractor consent regime (4.4), permit register (1.13/2.2), and record-keeping standards for Contemporary Records (20.1).

## Baseline Programme Review Notes Skeleton

> Programme Ref: **[rev/date]** — submitted under Sub-Clause 8.3.
> **Findings:** [1] Logic/sequencing issue — [activity refs]; [2] Obligation not represented — [clause/activity]; [3] Critical path unclear — [segment]; [4] Resource loading unsupported — [trade].
> **Decision:** Not accepted to the extent of the findings above; resubmit within [x] days addressing each numbered finding. Time for Completion assumptions to be confirmed.
> **Note:** Acceptance does not discharge the Contractor's obligations (8.3).

## QA Checklist Before Issuing

- [ ] Every security, guarantee and policy checked against Contract Data values and diarized for expiry
- [ ] Commencement Date notice issued in writing and logged in the notices register
- [ ] Programme review decision issued within the 21-day window with numbered, reasoned findings
- [ ] Contract Particulars Summary reconciled to the signed contract (no correspondence-only terms)
- [ ] Delegation matrix and correspondence matrix distributed to all parties
- [ ] Mobilization file indexed for audit (securities, insurances, notices, programme, manual)
